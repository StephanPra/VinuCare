About VinuCare

A Clinic That Feels Like Home for Your Pet

Founded in 2016, VinuCare is a full-service veterinary clinic and pet care centre dedicated to the health and happiness of every animal that walks through our doors.

Our team of licensed veterinarians, certified groomers and experienced caregivers work together to provide compassionate, evidence-based care for dogs, cats, birds, fish and small animals.
